About the app

Save the lookup. Keep the learning.

WordWise is an English vocabulary builder for learners who want to turn new words, idioms, and phrases into reusable learning data.

Instead of searching the same word again and again, learners can save explanations, organize vocabulary by context, and review the words that matter to them.

What it supports

Practical tools for real vocabulary habits.

AI-enhanced explanations

Look up vocabulary and save explanations that can reflect the learner's level and native language.

My Vocabulary

Keep a private vocabulary database of learning and learned words, idioms, phrases, and longer lookup text.

Tags and banks

Organize words by book, class, topic, exam, or goal, and browse curated vocabulary banks for focused study.

Flashcard review

Turn useful tag groups into decks and review due cards with a spaced-repetition style workflow.

Offline-friendly study

Review cached flashcards and view cached vocabulary after they have been loaded on the device.

Daily reminders

Use iPhone notifications to receive a daily reminder when flashcards are due for review.
